30 Other Resources

External textbooks, courses, software, and reference sites for causal inference and targeted learning. The list favours freely available, actively maintained resources.

30.1 Foundational textbooks (free online)

  • Hernán & Robins, Causal Inference: What If – The standard graduate text. Potential outcomes, DAGs, g-methods, and longitudinal causal inference, with the full PDF available at no cost.
  • van der Laan & Rose, Targeted Learning (2011) and Targeted Learning in Data Science (2018) – The comprehensive references for the TMLE and Super Learner framework.
  • Kennedy, Semiparametric doubly robust targeted double machine learning: a review (2022) – A concise modern review of the efficiency theory behind doubly robust and targeted estimators, for readers who want the theory behind the influence-function machinery.

30.2 Online courses and interactive tutorials

  • Beyond the ATE – Interactive workshop on modified treatment policies and the lmtp package (Nick Williams and Iván Díaz).
  • TLverse Handbook (Targeted Learning in R) – Book-length guide to the targeted-learning software ecosystem (sl3, tmle3, origami, hal9001).
  • Introduction to Modern Causal Inference – Accessible, code-first introduction to causal inference concepts (Alejandro Schuler).
  • Visual Guides to Causal Inference – Illustrated guides to TMLE, Super Learner, and modified treatment policies (Katherine Hoffman).
  • Causal Diagrams: Draw Your Assumptions Before Your Conclusions – Free Harvard/edX course on DAGs (Miguel Hernán).
  • Introduction to Causal Inference – Free lecture course with downloadable notes (Brady Neal).
  • A Crash Course in Causality – Free Coursera course on confounding, IPW, and g-methods for an epidemiology audience (Jason Roy).

30.3 Applied complements

  • Causal Inference: The Mixtape – Applied, econometrics-flavoured treatment of causal designs (Scott Cunningham).
  • Causal Inference for the Brave and True – Python-based applied causal inference handbook (Matheus Facure).

30.4 Software

Maintenance and channel status below are current as of mid-2026; because that status is the most perishable part of any software list, confirm a package’s version and channel before relying on it. A fuller treatment of the targeted-learning package landscape, with versions, download counts, and recommendations by estimand, is given in the TMLE software scoping review (Mertens and colleagues).

Core estimators (R, on CRAN).

  • tmle – point-treatment ATE, risk ratio, odds ratio, and marginal structural model parameters; the reference implementation.
  • drtmle – point-treatment counterfactual means with doubly robust inference (intervals stay valid when one nuisance converges slowly).
  • ltmle – longitudinal TMLE for time-varying treatment and confounding; reports the g-computation and IPW estimates alongside the targeted one.
  • lmtp – longitudinal modified treatment policies (static, dynamic, and feasible shifts), point and longitudinal, including survival with competing risks. Released under AGPL-3, which can matter for closed-source or commercial reuse.
  • txshift – population mean under an additive shift of a continuous exposure, with two-phase sampling.
  • SuperLearner – cross-validated ensemble for nuisance estimation.

Specialized settings.

  • Survival and competing risks: survtmle (discrete-time cumulative incidence) and concrete (continuous-time absolute risk). Both are archived from CRAN and now install from GitHub; lmtp is the maintained CRAN alternative for survival with competing risks.
  • Mediation: crumble and medoutcon – interventional direct and indirect effects (GitHub).
  • Near-positivity violations: ctmle – collaborative TMLE (CRAN; not updated since 2019, so confirm it builds).
  • Optimal individualized rules: tmle3mopttx – the only dedicated targeted-learning implementation (GitHub / r-universe). For exploratory CATE, grf and policytree.
  • Combining a trial with real-world data: EScvtmle (experiment-selector CV-TMLE) and atmle (adaptive TMLE; GitHub).
  • High-dimensional biomarker importance: biotmle (Bioconductor).

Infrastructure (R).

  • hal9001 – highly adaptive lasso; origami – cross-validation; haldensify – HAL conditional density estimation for shift-intervention nuisances. The tlverse ecosystem (sl3, tmle3) ties these together.

Python.

  • causalml and zEpid – point-treatment TMLE for the ATE; pytmle – survival and competing risks. No Python package yet spans longitudinal, modified-treatment-policy, mediation, or high-dimensional estimands, so use the R tools for those.

Other environments.

  • Stata: the contributed eltmle command (point-treatment ATE).

Diagnostics and sensitivity (used in this handbook).

  • EValue, episensr, ipw.

30.5 Research groups

  • UC Berkeley Center for Targeted Machine Learning and Causal Inference (CTML) – Center developing the methods and software in this handbook.
  • CAUSALab, Harvard – Target-trial emulation and g-methods.

30.6 Workshops and community

  • TMLE Workshop (Ehsan Karim) – Applied TMLE workshop materials with R code.

# Other Resources

External textbooks, courses, software, and reference sites for causal inference and targeted learning. The list favours freely available, actively maintained resources.

## Foundational textbooks (free online)

- [Hernán & Robins, *Causal Inference: What If*](https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/miguel-hernan/causal-inference-book/) -- The standard graduate text. Potential outcomes, DAGs, g-methods, and longitudinal causal inference, with the full PDF available at no cost.
- [van der Laan & Rose, *Targeted Learning* (2011)](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4419-9782-1) and [*Targeted Learning in Data Science* (2018)](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-65304-4) -- The comprehensive references for the TMLE and Super Learner framework.
- [Kennedy, *Semiparametric doubly robust targeted double machine learning: a review* (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.06469) -- A concise modern review of the efficiency theory behind doubly robust and targeted estimators, for readers who want the theory behind the influence-function machinery.

## Online courses and interactive tutorials

- [Beyond the ATE](https://beyondtheate.com) -- Interactive workshop on modified treatment policies and the `lmtp` package (Nick Williams and Iván Díaz).
- [TLverse Handbook (*Targeted Learning in R*)](https://tlverse.org/tlverse-handbook/) -- Book-length guide to the targeted-learning software ecosystem (`sl3`, `tmle3`, `origami`, `hal9001`).
- [Introduction to Modern Causal Inference](https://alejandroschuler.github.io/mci/) -- Accessible, code-first introduction to causal inference concepts (Alejandro Schuler).
- [Visual Guides to Causal Inference](https://www.khstats.com/) -- Illustrated guides to TMLE, Super Learner, and modified treatment policies (Katherine Hoffman).
- [Causal Diagrams: Draw Your Assumptions Before Your Conclusions](https://www.edx.org/learn/data-analysis/harvard-university-causal-diagrams-draw-your-assumptions-before-your-conclusions) -- Free Harvard/edX course on DAGs (Miguel Hernán).
- [Introduction to Causal Inference](https://www.bradyneal.com/causal-inference-course) -- Free lecture course with downloadable notes (Brady Neal).
- [A Crash Course in Causality](https://www.coursera.org/learn/crash-course-in-causality) -- Free Coursera course on confounding, IPW, and g-methods for an epidemiology audience (Jason Roy).

## Applied complements

- [Causal Inference: The Mixtape](https://mixtape.scunning.com/) -- Applied, econometrics-flavoured treatment of causal designs (Scott Cunningham).
- [Causal Inference for the Brave and True](https://matheusfacure.github.io/python-causality-handbook/) -- Python-based applied causal inference handbook (Matheus Facure).

## Software

Maintenance and channel status below are current as of mid-2026; because that status is the most perishable part of any software list, confirm a package's version and channel before relying on it. A fuller treatment of the targeted-learning package landscape, with versions, download counts, and recommendations by estimand, is given in the TMLE software scoping review (Mertens and colleagues).

**Core estimators (R, on CRAN).**

- [`tmle`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=tmle) -- point-treatment ATE, risk ratio, odds ratio, and marginal structural model parameters; the reference implementation.
- [`drtmle`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=drtmle) -- point-treatment counterfactual means with doubly robust inference (intervals stay valid when one nuisance converges slowly).
- [`ltmle`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=ltmle) -- longitudinal TMLE for time-varying treatment and confounding; reports the g-computation and IPW estimates alongside the targeted one.
- [`lmtp`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=lmtp) -- longitudinal modified treatment policies (static, dynamic, and feasible shifts), point and longitudinal, including survival with competing risks. Released under AGPL-3, which can matter for closed-source or commercial reuse.
- [`txshift`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=txshift) -- population mean under an additive shift of a continuous exposure, with two-phase sampling.
- [`SuperLearner`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=SuperLearner) -- cross-validated ensemble for nuisance estimation.

**Specialized settings.**

- Survival and competing risks: [`survtmle`](https://github.com/benkeser/survtmle) (discrete-time cumulative incidence) and [`concrete`](https://github.com/imbroglio-dc/concrete) (continuous-time absolute risk). Both are archived from CRAN and now install from GitHub; `lmtp` is the maintained CRAN alternative for survival with competing risks.
- Mediation: [`crumble`](https://github.com/nt-williams/crumble) and [`medoutcon`](https://github.com/nt-williams/medoutcon) -- interventional direct and indirect effects (GitHub).
- Near-positivity violations: [`ctmle`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=ctmle) -- collaborative TMLE (CRAN; not updated since 2019, so confirm it builds).
- Optimal individualized rules: [`tmle3mopttx`](https://github.com/tlverse/tmle3mopttx) -- the only dedicated targeted-learning implementation (GitHub / r-universe). For exploratory CATE, [`grf`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=grf) and `policytree`.
- Combining a trial with real-world data: [`EScvtmle`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=EScvtmle) (experiment-selector CV-TMLE) and `atmle` (adaptive TMLE; GitHub).
- High-dimensional biomarker importance: [`biotmle`](https://bioconductor.org/packages/biotmle) (Bioconductor).

**Infrastructure (R).**

- [`hal9001`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=hal9001) -- highly adaptive lasso; [`origami`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=origami) -- cross-validation; [`haldensify`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=haldensify) -- HAL conditional density estimation for shift-intervention nuisances. The [tlverse](https://github.com/tlverse) ecosystem (`sl3`, `tmle3`) ties these together.

**Python.**

- [`causalml`](https://pypi.org/project/causalml/) and [`zEpid`](https://pypi.org/project/zepid/) -- point-treatment TMLE for the ATE; [`pytmle`](https://pypi.org/project/pytmle/) -- survival and competing risks. No Python package yet spans longitudinal, modified-treatment-policy, mediation, or high-dimensional estimands, so use the R tools for those.

**Other environments.**

- Stata: the contributed `eltmle` command (point-treatment ATE).

**Diagnostics and sensitivity (used in this handbook).**

- [`EValue`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=EValue), [`episensr`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=episensr), [`ipw`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=ipw).
